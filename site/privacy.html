Privacy policy

Effective date: 22 November 2026

nguda is a desktop planner and time tracker for macOS and Linux. It runs entirely on your computer. This policy explains what data nguda accesses, how it uses and stores that data, and how you can delete it.

Summary

  • Your data stays on your computer. nguda has no servers and no accounts.
  • Google Calendar access is optional and read-only.
  • nguda does not share, sell, or transfer your data to anyone.
  • There is no telemetry, analytics, or advertising.

What nguda accesses

Data you enter. Your tasks, estimates, plan, and time log, which you create in the app.

Google Calendar, only if you sign in. nguda requests these two read-only scopes:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.calendarlist.readonly: reads the list of your calendars, so you can choose which ones go into today's plan.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events.readonly: reads the events of the calendars you chose, so nguda can place them in today's plan.

nguda never creates, changes, or deletes anything in your Google Calendar.

How nguda uses it

nguda uses your calendar list only to show you the calendars you can choose from, and uses the events of the chosen calendars only to place them in your daily plan and summary inside the app. Google user data is not used for advertising, is not used to train any model, and is not read by any person.

Storage

  • Your plan, tasks, time log, and imported calendar items are stored locally in a SQLite database, nguda.sqlite3, in the app data folder:
    • macOS: ~/Library/Application Support/dev.nguda.app/
    • Linux: $XDG_DATA_HOME/dev.nguda.app/ (default ~/.local/share/dev.nguda.app/)
  • The Google refresh token is kept in your operating system's keyring (macOS Keychain, Linux Secret Service). If no keyring is available, the sign-in is kept in memory for the current session only and is gone when nguda quits.

Network access

The only network traffic nguda makes is to Google, for sign-in and calendar sync. No data is sent to the developers of nguda or to any other party.

Sharing

None. nguda does not share, sell, rent, or transfer your data, including any information received from Google APIs, to anyone.

Telemetry

None. nguda has no telemetry, analytics, crash reporting, or advertising.

Google API Services User Data Policy

nguda's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data and revoking access

  • Sign out in the window's Google Calendar section. This deletes the refresh token from the keyring. Items already imported stay in your plan until you delete them.
  • Delete the app data folder listed under Storage to remove all local data, including imported calendar items.
  • Revoke access to nguda in your Google Account at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new effective date. The history of this page is public in the source repository.

Contact

Questions about this policy or about your data: open an issue at https://github.com/purwa-astawa/nguda-app/issues.
